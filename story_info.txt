TITLE: The Deer and the Firefly Path
MORAL: When we stay kind and calm, even a small light can help someone reach home safely.
TARGET_MINUTES: 7
TELUGU_STYLE: Modern spoken Telugu that parents and children use today. Keep words simple and natural. Avoid old, literary, or hard words. Prefer everyday words like అమ్మ, ఇల్లు, దారి, భయం పెట్టుకోకు, నెమ్మదిగా, మనం కలిసి వెళ్దాం.
DESCRIPTION: On a new moon night, the forest is extra dark and quiet. A gentle deer hears a tiny baby rabbit crying near the bushes because she cannot find the way back to her mommy. The deer promises to help. Fireflies gather one by one and make a soft golden path. Along the way, the deer teaches the rabbit to breathe slowly, listen carefully, and trust kind friends. They pass a stream, tall grass, and a sleepy banyan tree. At last, the baby rabbit reaches her mommy, and the forest settles into a peaceful goodnight.
